# 01-ugc-script-prompt.md

Act as an expert UGC ad copywriter and direct-response marketer.

Create a 30-second UGC-style video advertisement for the following gym:

Business Name: Arise GYM

Target Audience:

* Students
* Working professionals
* Beginners looking to lose weight or gain muscle

Platform:
Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts

Tone:
Authentic, motivating, and relatable.

Structure:

1. Hook (first 3 seconds)
2. Problem
3. Solution
4. Personal experience/testimonial style
5. Call-to-Action

Requirements:

* Sound like a real gym member, not a company.
* Focus on transformation and consistency.
* Keep the script conversational.
* Include natural pauses and emotions.
* End with a strong CTA encouraging a gym visit or trial membership.
